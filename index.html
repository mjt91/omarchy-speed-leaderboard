Nine days after DHH declared 50 seconds the record, an r/omarchy post beat it by four — without claiming anything. The proof is a phone photo of the completion screen rather than a screenshot, and the poster stated no hardware at all.

How it got here

Omarchy went from a five-minute install to under a minute in roughly a year. Most of that came from one decision: making the installer work offline, which took the network out of the equation and left the disk as the thing that decides your number.

  • 2025-08-25 · Omarchy 2.0
    Under five minutes, with a fast connection

    DHH publishes the breakdown: one minute formatting and encrypting the drive, one minute installing Arch, two minutes forty installing and configuring Omarchy itself. The install still needs the network, so bandwidth is part of everyone's number.

    @dhh on X

  • 2025-09-08 · Omarchy 3.0 RC
    Under three minutes

    Ryan Hughes rebuilds the install flow around a new offline ISO. Removing the network removes the largest source of variance between machines — from here on, an install time is a statement about your disk, not your ISP.

    @dhh on X

  • 2025-09-10 · Omarchy 3.0 RC
    Two minutes

    Two days later, the same work lands at two minutes flat for a full install of a complete, configured operating system.

    @dhh on X

  • 2026-08-14 · Omarchy 4.0 (Quattro)
    Sub-minute becomes possible

    The Quattro release notes claim installation is over 30% faster and the ISO more than a gigabyte smaller, at under 6GB — with sub-minute installs now possible. Days later, an HP ZBook Ultra G1a turns the claim into 50 seconds.

    Omarchy 4.0.0 release notes

About the times on this page

Every time here is the number Omarchy's own installer printed on its completion screen — Installed Omarchy in 0m 46s — not a stopwatch held by us or anyone else.

These times are taken on trust. That screen shows one line of text and nothing else: no version, no hardware, no date. A screenshot of it proves nothing, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Versions and specs are self-reported. Runs marked Observed in the wild were collected from public posts by people who never agreed to any of this.

Got a faster one? Open an issue with your completion screen and your drive.
